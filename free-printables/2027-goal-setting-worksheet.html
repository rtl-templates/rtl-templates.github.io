How to set goals you will actually keep

  1. Start with a check-in, not a wish list. Rating each area shows where a small change would help most.
  2. Pick three goals at most. Ten resolutions fade by February. Three you can keep in mind every week.
  3. Write the why. On a hard day, the reason is what keeps you going.
  4. Make the first step tiny. "Walk 10 minutes after lunch" beats "get fit".
  5. Plan by month. Fill in one small step per month, then check it off at the end of the month.
