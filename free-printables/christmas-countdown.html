Free Christmas Countdown Printable

An advent activity calendar with one small, cozy thing to do each day from December 1 to 24. Most of the ideas cost nothing. A blank version is included so you can write your own. No sign-up, just download.

  2. Make paper snowflakes for the windows
  3. Christmas movie night with popcorn
  4. Bake and decorate Christmas cookies
  5. Go and see the Christmas lights
  6. Make a homemade ornament
  7. Donate toys or warm clothes
  8. Read a Christmas story at bedtime
  9. Hot chocolate with marshmallows
  10. Build a gingerbread house
  11. Send cards to grandparents
  12. Christmas pajama dance party
  13. Build a blanket fort and read
  14. Make a paper chain for the tree
  15. Sing carols together
  16. Family game night
  17. Hang a bird feeder for winter birds
  18. Wrap presents together
  19. Leave a thank-you treat for the mail carrier
  20. Christmas scavenger hunt at home
  21. Do a random act of kindness
  22. Make a card for a neighbor
  23. Bake treats and leave a carrot for the reindeer
  24. Read The Night Before Christmas

How to use it

Print the calendar and stick it on the fridge. Each morning, read out the day's activity, then color in the circle when it is done. Swap days around when the week gets busy, since the order does not matter. For older kids, use the blank page and let them pick their own 24 ideas.
