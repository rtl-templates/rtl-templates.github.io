How to play Christmas bingo

  1. Give each player a card and some markers: buttons, mini marshmallows or chocolate chips.
  2. Cut the caller sheet into slips and put them in a hat, or tick the words off on the sheet as you call them.
  3. Players cover each word they have. The free square in the middle counts for everyone.
  4. The first player with five in a row, across, down or diagonally, shouts "Bingo!" and wins a small prize.

Tip: for a longer game, play "blackout", where the winner is the first to cover the whole card.
