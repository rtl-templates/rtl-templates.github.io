Tips that make it stick

Name what you are saving for, set an automatic transfer on payday, and color in a box every time you save. If you miss a week, just pick any open amount that fits. The order does not matter, the total does.

Then pay off debt with the same habit

When a challenge is done, roll the same weekly amount into your smallest debt (snowball) or your highest interest rate (avalanche). The debt pages walk you through both.
